Dinner List — version 1

Open Dinner_List_App.html in a modern browser.

What it does:
- Stores meals in your browser's local storage.
- Shows all recipes grouped by cuisine.
- Lets you add and edit meals.
- Tracks Not Made / Pending / Made.
- Chooses up to 7 Not Made meals while trying to keep the remaining pool balanced across:
  main type, meat level, fried level, soup, Claire rating bands, ease bands, and cuisine.
- Lets you mulligan one meal or all seven.
- Marks an accepted seven as Pending.
- Resets all statuses to Not Made only after confirmation.

Important:
The recipes are saved in that browser on that device, not inside the HTML file.
Clearing browser/site data can erase them. This version intentionally does not include
accounts, cloud sync, analytics, calendars, or other extras.
